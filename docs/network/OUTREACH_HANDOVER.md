Generated from the capability registry and the live message composer by

## What this is

A list of research groups and services that, from their **public** work, look able to answer
one of the open questions about this child's biology — and, for each, the exact question we
would put to them and the smallest useful thing they could send back.

**Nothing here has been sent.** External sending is switched off in this software. Every
message below is a prepared draft. Whether to contact anyone, and who signs the message, is
a decision for the treating clinician and the family, not for this project.

Each entry says plainly what is **public fact** about that group and what remains
**unconfirmed**. A group appearing here has not agreed to anything, has not been asked, and
may hold nothing relevant. Their listed capability is what their own publications and pages
say, not a commitment.

```

Dear Research Team at CHU de Québec–Université Laval,

Clinical Leadership: Unassigned (no clinical lead is currently appointed). This is an exploratory preclinical research inquiry; no clinical leadership or patient-care decision is asserted or implied.

1. Bounded Research Question:
We are investigating a specific preclinical question (Q01):
Question: In an appropriately characterized model, what function remains when altered BubR1 abundance is normalized?
What an answer could change: Whether an abundance-restoration premise remains plausible; which functional endpoints remain necessary
Target Endpoint: BubR1 residual function

2. Relevance and Public Source:
Your institution was identified based on public scientific publications and institutional profiles:
Institution: CHU de Québec–Université Laval (Elowe lab)
Public Reference: https://www.projets-recherche.ulaval.ca/projet?id=479868
Relevant Public Capabilities: Mitotic kinase signaling, BubR1 phosphorylation, and checkpoint silencing research; Cell-based functional assays for chromosome alignment and spindle checkpoint timing; Structure-function analysis of BubR1 pseudokinase domain and functional motifs

3. Smallest Useful Contribution:
We seek the smallest useful contribution that can inform this question. A complete study, private data transfer, or biological specimen shipment is NOT required. We welcome any of the following alternatives:
  (a) An existing result or historical finding relevant to the mechanism.
  (b) A brief methods explanation, interpretation, or advice on assay limitations.
  (c) A referral to another group, sample custodian, or research infrastructure.
  (d) A feasibility reply on whether your laboratory could evaluate this endpoint.
  (e) A formal quote or service agreement proposal.

4. Disclosure Limits:
Preclinical research scope only. Under institutional policy, no identifying patient combinations, exact variant coordinates, private repository paths, or clinical case records are disclosed.

5. Declining Further Contact:
If your group does not wish to participate, does not have relevant resources, or prefers not to receive further contact, please reply with 'DECLINE' or 'OPT OUT'. We will immediately respect your preference and close this outreach route without further follow-up.

Sincerely,
<the accountable sender's address>
```

Every message is built the same way: it names the sender and their role, states one bounded
question, says why this group specifically, offers five ways to reply including a plain
referral or a short explanation, states what we will not disclose, and gives a one-line way
to decline and stop all further contact.

## What is deliberately not in the message

- The child's exact genetic variants, their position, or anything identifying them.
- Any request for a sample, a dataset, or patient records.
- Any claim that a clinical lead has been appointed when none has.
- Any suggestion of treatment. These are laboratory questions about cells.
